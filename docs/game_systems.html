6. Công thức tính damage

Damage = (base_dmg × skill_mult + flat_bonus) × crit_mult × armor_mult × random_range

Các bước

  1. Sát thương cơ bản: dmg_base = random(base_dmg_min, base_dmg_max)
  2. Nhân hệ số skill: dmg_skill = dmg_base × skill_multiplier
  3. Cộng flat bonus (từ item, buff): dmg_buffed = dmg_skill + flat_damage_bonus
  4. Check crit:
    • crit_chance = base_crit + crit_buffs - enemy_crit_resist (clamp [0%, 50%])
    • random(0,1) < crit_chance → crit_mult = 1.5, else 1.0
  5. Giáp enemy: dmg_after_armor = max(1, dmg_buffed - enemy_armor)
  6. Random variance: final = round(dmg_after_armor × random(0.95, 1.05))
  7. Stress dmg lên hero: stress_dmg = (skill_stress + hero_stress_bonus) × (1 - enemy_stress_resist)

Ví dụ minh họa

Hero: Warrior, base_dmg=8, base_crit=8%
Skill: Shield Slam, dmg_mult=1.2, stress_dmg=5
Enemy: Goblin, armor=0, stress_resist=10%

→ dmg_base   = random(6,10) = 9
→ dmg_skill  = 9 × 1.2 = 10.8
→ dmg_buffed = 10.8 (no flat bonus)
→ crit       = false (random > 0.08)
→ dmg_armor  = 10.8 - 0 = 10.8
→ final      = round(10.8 × 1.02) = 11 HP
→ stress     = 5 × (1 - 0.1) = 4.5 → 5 stress
